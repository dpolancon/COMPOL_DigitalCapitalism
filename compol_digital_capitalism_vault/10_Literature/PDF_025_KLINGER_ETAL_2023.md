*   **Bibliographic Identity:**
    *   **Authors:** Ulrike Klinger, Karolina Koc-Michalska, and Uta Russmann.
    *   **Title:** "Are Campaigns Getting Uglier, and Who Is to Blame? Negativity, Dramatization and Populism on Facebook in the 2014 and 2019 EP Election Campaigns".
    *   **Journal/Venue:** Identified by running head as *Dissonant Public Spheres* (Note: Likely published in *Digital Journalism* or a related special issue based on text context, but header provided says "Dissonant Public Spheres").
    *   **Year:** Post-2021 (Inferred from citations like Koc-Michalska et al., 2021 and EP 2019 results analysis).
    *   **Article Type:** Empirical research article (Quantitative content analysis).
*   **What the Source Studies:** The relationship between negative emotional content, dramatization, and populist messaging in political party Facebook posts across the European Parliament (EP) elections.
*   **Method:** Quantitative content analysis.
*   **Corpus:** 14,293 posts from 111 political parties (2014) and 116 political parties (2019) across 12 countries.
*   **Core Claims:**

    3.  This trend is the result of strategic campaigning, not just unintentional digital affordances.
