These academic sources investigate how **modern political actors** leverage **media dynamics** and **digital technology** to influence public perception. One study examines the impact of **deepfakes**—AI-generated videos—finding that while they successfully damage a **politician’s reputation**, their effectiveness is significantly increased when combined with **microtargeting** to reach specific, vulnerable audiences. A second study focuses on **right-wing populism**, demonstrating how leaders use **deliberate provocations** to force journalists into providing **scandalizing coverage**. Although the resulting news is negative, it grants these parties the **"oxygen of publicity,"** effectively boosting their **visibility** and **public awareness**. Together, the research highlights a shift where **manipulated content** and **norm violations** are strategically used to navigate and exploit the **modern information ecosystem**. These findings suggest that **malicious communication strategies** can undermine democratic discourse by prioritizing **attention-grabbing tactics** over factual debate.
