### 2. Source Analysis (Studies, Method, Corpus)
*   **What the source studies:** The sociopolitical contradictions of digital labor on Twitter, specifically focusing on the tension between user agency/liberation and platform exploitation/control. It analyzes the 2017 shutdown of NYC digital news sites (Gothamist/DNAinfo) and the concurrent change in Twitter's governance.
*   **Method:** Critical theoretical reflection and commentary. It uses Mary Boo Anderson’s "expanding brain" artwork as a heuristic device to reflect on Kylie Jarrett’s Marxist feminist framework. It employs a case study approach regarding specific Twitter rule changes (Nov 2017) and interface modifications.
*   **Corpus:**
    *   Twitter social data (330M user base estimate).
    *   Specific Twitter event: Nov 3, 2017 Mary Boo Anderson tweet.
    *   Corporate action: Joe Ricketts' shutdown of Gothamist/DNAinfo (Nov 2017).
    *   Platform governance texts: Twitter Rules (specifically the removal of "speaking truth to power").
    *   Theoretical texts: Kylie Jarrett’s *The Digital Housewife* and *Feminism, Labour and Digital Media*.

### 3. Core Claims
*   **Digital Labor is Exploitative:** Twitter users and content creators perform "unpaid cognitive and communicative labour" that generates value for the platform (exemplified by the shutdown of journalists vs. the unpaid content creation of users).
*   **Platform Governance Shifts:** The removal of "speaking truth to power" from Twitter rules and the increase of tweet length (140 to 280 chars) signal a shift from facilitating public discourse to prioritizing corporate control and user retention over radical expression.
*   **The "Social Factory":** Capitalism expands its expropriating logics into the social domain; communicative labor (ideas, identities) is generated within this framework.
*   **Contradictions:** Digital labor is simultaneously oppressive (exploitation) and liberatory (marginalized ideas gaining prominence, e.g., Anderson's meme).
