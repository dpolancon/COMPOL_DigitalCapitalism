### **1. Bibliographic & Methodological Extraction**

*   **Bibliographic Identity:**
    *   **Title:** Between Hagiography and Self-Trolling: Multimodal Analysis of Memes for Boric in the 2021 Chilean Presidential Election.
    *   **Authors:** Mario Álvarez Fuentes (Universidad de La Frontera) & Claudia Mellado (Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso).
    *   **Journal:** *International Journal of Communication* (IJOC), Vol. 18, 2024.
*   **What the Source Studies:**
    *   Political memes (video and image) related to candidate Gabriel Boric during the 2021 Chilean Presidential Election.
    *   Specifically, how supporters constructed a narrative of the election and Boric’s candidacy using humor and absurdity.
*   **Method:**
    *   **Qualitative Multimodal Analysis:** Based on Kress and Van Leeuwen’s (2006) principles of visual discourse analysis (Ideational, Interpersonal, Textual meta-functions).
    *   **Data Collection:** Curated sampling from specific social media and private channels.
*   **Corpus:**
    *   **Size:** 201 final memes (180 images, 21 videos).
    *   **Timeframe:** Week after the first round of the 2021 election (Dec 2021).
    *   **Sources:** Twitter (Shitpost Boris, Gamba.cl, The Clinic), Facebook, Instagram, and researchers' personal instant messaging accounts.
    *   **Exclusions:** 22 duplicates and 25 non-humorous campaign materials (portraits with "Vote Boric" text).
*   **Core Claims:**
    *   Memes utilize **absurdity** to create a "hagiographic" narrative (heroic idealization) of Boric.
    *   This process functions as **self-trolling**, creating a sense of community among supporters by validating their shared interpretation of the political moment.
    *   Memes are not just content but "lures" that build "interpretive communities" through intertextuality and replication.

It offers comparative data on how meme culture differs from traditional campaign messaging.

    *   **Sample Bias:** The corpus was curated from accounts known for satire/specific support (Shitpost Boris, The Clinic), potentially excluding critical or neutral memes.
    *   **Time Bound:** Limited to the immediate post-first-round week; does not cover the full campaign or post-election governance.

    *   *Specific Accounts:* Shitpost Boris, Gamba.cl, The Clinic.

    *   **Intertextuality:** Connecting candidates to broader political culture.
