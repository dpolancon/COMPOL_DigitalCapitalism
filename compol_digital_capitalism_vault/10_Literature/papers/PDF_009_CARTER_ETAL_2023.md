### 1. Bibliographic Identity & General Analysis

*   **Bibliographic Identity:**
    *   **Title:** A replication and extension of the Personal Social Media Ecosystem Framework
    *   **Authors:** Michael C. Carter, Drew P. Cingel, Samantha L. Vigil, Jeanette B. Ruiz
    *   **Journal:** *Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication*
    *   **Year:** 2023
    *   **DOI:** 10.1093/jcmc/zmad036
    *   **Institution:** University of California, Davis (Lead) / Boston Children's Hospital

*   **What the Source Studies:**
    *   The study investigates **User Interface Classes (UICs)** within the **Personal Social Media Ecosystem Framework (PSMEF)**.
    *   It aims to extend the PSMEF by identifying distinct types of online spaces (interfaces) users navigate across six major platforms (Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, Twitter, TikTok, YouTube).
    *   It seeks to validate these classes quantitatively using text analysis and semantic networks.

*   **Method and Corpus:**
    *   **Method:** Mixed methods. Specifically, **Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)** (topic modeling) and **Schematic Semantic Network Analysis (SSNA)**.
    *   **Corpus:** Open-ended survey responses from **N=237** college students (California) detailing the pages/sections they navigate on social media. Initial N=380, screened to N=237.
    *   **Geographic Scope:** United States (Public university in California).
    *   **Demographics:** Ages 18–34 (M=19.41), majority Asian (57%), female (71%).

*   **Core Claims:**
    *   There are four distinct classes of user interfaces (e.g., Home Pages, Search Pages, Overtly Algorithmic Content Pages).
    *   The PSMEF can be extended via the **Digital User Interface Model**, which conceptualizes interfaces as spatial mental representations.
    *   Interface classes are consistent across platforms (e.g., a "Profile" on Instagram functions similarly to a "Profile" on Facebook in terms of user mental mapping).

### 2. Methodological Extraction (G2_TOPIC_MODELING_METHOD)

*   **Method Type:** **LDA** (Latent Dirichlet Allocation). *Note: The text explicitly states LDA, not STM or BERTopic.*
*   **Corpus:** Open-ended text descriptions provided by participants about the pages/sections they navigate (e.g., "my feed on Instagram").
*   **Validation Strategy:** **Schematic Semantic Network Analysis (SSNA)** used alongside LDA to model interface groupings quantitatively based on a shared understanding of composition.
*   **Preprocessing Choices:**
    *   Screening for entries designating specific in-app pages/sections.
    *   Use of syntax (e.g., colons) to demarcate sections.
    *   Multiple entries screened out (e.g., Instagram N=145 screened out due to failure to provide a designated label).
*   **Spanish/Short-text/Political-ad Text Handling:**
    *   **Not Applicable.** The corpus consists of English paragraphs of description, not short text (tweets), Spanish, or political advertisements.
    *   **Handling of Short Text:** The text describes "paragraphs" of description, not micro-blogging text.
*   **Method Limitations:**
    *   LDA requires text volume; the study screened for sufficient depth in description.
    *   Self-report bias in describing interface features.
    *   Topic modeling (LDA) assumes topics are independent; SSNA was used to validate the semantic structure.
