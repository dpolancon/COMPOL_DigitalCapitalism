### 2. Theoretical Object

*   **Theoretical Object:** Big Data Capitalism, Digital Labour, Digital Commodities, and the "General Intellect."
*   **Power Mechanism:** Exploitation of unpaid and underpaid digital labor; commodification of information and communication; control via algorithms and machinery; appropriation of the "general social knowledge."
*   **Political Advertising as Platform-Mediated Infrastructure:** *(Note: This specific text does not explicitly theorize political advertising as infrastructure.)* However, it implies the infrastructure of **communication**. Fuchs argues communication is the way humans relate symbolically, which becomes a commodity ("commodification of communication"). Political advertising would fall under the "commodification of digital content and online audiences."
*   **Portable Concepts:**
    1.  **Dialectic of Technology:** Technology in itself (essence) vs. Technology in Capitalism (existence).
    2.  **General Intellect:** Knowledge and communication as a direct force of production.
    3.  **Antagonism of Commodification:** The tension between digital capital and the digital commons.
*   **What to Exclude:** Neoliberal policy specifics, non-Marxist communication theories (only used for contrast, e.g., Habermas, Luhmann), and specific technical mechanics of ad servers.
*   **3 Conceptual Building Blocks:**
    1.  **The Commodity Form:** Information and labor are turned into commodities, creating a contradiction between the digital commons and digital capital.
    2.  **Digital Labour:** Exploitation of workers in the International Division of Digital Labour (IDDL), including unpaid work.
    3.  **Productive Forces vs. Relations of Production:** The tension between networked digital technologies (forces) and private capitalist ownership (relations).

*   **Definition of Digitalization/Platformization:** Defined as "Big Data Capitalism," where quantity turns into quality. It is the integration of digital data streams into the capital accumulation process.
