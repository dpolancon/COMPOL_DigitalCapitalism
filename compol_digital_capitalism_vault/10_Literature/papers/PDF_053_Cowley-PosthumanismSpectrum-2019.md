### 2. Theoretical Extraction
*   **Theoretical Object:** The "Posthuman Subject" (or the lack thereof). Specifically, the "posthuman sensibility" and "hybrid thinking."
*   **Power Mechanism:** **Ontological Privileging of Hybridity.** Power operates by normalizing the conditions of production where human agency is "eroded" or "displaced" by digital mediation.
*   **Political Advertising as Infrastructure:** *Not addressed in this text.* The text focuses on theory, not campaign mechanics.
*   **Portable Concepts:**
    *   **Posthumanism as a Spectrum:** A flexible analytical tool rather than a rigid ideology.
    *   **Contingency:** Treating theoretical narratives as produced by specific circumstances rather than universal truths.
    *   **Affirmative Ethico-political Argument:** Reframing loss (end of modernity) as opportunity.
*   **What to Exclude:**
    *   Exclusion of "Universalist aspirations" (Posthumanism is not a universal truth).
    *   Exclusion of "Aggressive Nihilists" (those who cynically refute dilemmas for gain).
    *   Exclusion of pure "Poststructuralism" (Posthumanism is distinct from linguistic deconstruction).
*   **3 Conceptual Building Blocks:**
    1.  **The End of Modernity:** The dispiriting narrative of human loss of control (Anthropocene).
    2.  **Hybridity:** The blurring of boundaries between human/non-human, natural/cultural.
    3.  **Affirmative Optimization:** Using the "crisis" to empower alternative schemes of thought and representation.
