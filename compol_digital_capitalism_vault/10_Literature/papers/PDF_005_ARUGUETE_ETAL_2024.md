### 2. Country/Election Context (G4)
*   **Country/Election Studied:** **Chile**, specifically **2020-2021** (during the pandemic and municipal elections).
*   **Distinctive Context:**
    *   **Post-2019 Uprising:** The study takes place immediately after the massive social uprising (October 2019) where national legitimacy was at its lowest.
    *   **Trust Shift:** Mayors emerged as the "most trusted political figures" in contrast to the national government and opposition.
    *   **Lockdowns:** Strict lockdown measures forced local governments to rely on digital channels.
    *   **Decentralization:** Public health strategy was municipal-level (decentralized lockdowns + vaccination).
*   **LatAm Distinctiveness:** Highlights Chile's higher vaccination percentage (per Mathieu et al., 2020) and the specific political crisis context of the 2019-2021 period.
*   **Platforms Used:** **Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram**.
*   **Contrast Case:** Mentions **Italy** (earthquake crisis) in footnote 1, but the primary contrast is between **National vs. Local** levels of government.
*   **Justification Sentence for Chile as Theoretical Case:**
    > "Studying social media messaging in this context of strict lockdown and of the heightened prominence of all mayors... enables us to better isolate the influence of structural factors on mayors' political communication strategies."
